How to read this page — and a correction from July 29 ▸

Most numbers here are estimates, not polls. Only of 432 rated districts has a real public poll. Individual House districts are barely polled, so the rest are rating-derived — drawn faded and tagged rating throughout. The one genuinely dated series is the national generic ballot.

Correction, July 29. An earlier version reported a national average of D +1.18 and a Democratic range of 222–244 seats. Those were wrong — they came from a source file with scrambled party labels, which listed Rep. Robert Aderholt (R-AL) as a Democrat and rated Terri Sewell's safe Democratic seat as Solid Republican. districts were mislabelled. Rebuilt from Election Center's own ratings snapshot (212 D-side / 220 R-side, matching the current chamber). The corrected projection points the other way: Democrats land at seats.
